How to play

Click a blue-ringed unit → green tiles = reachable (move cost: plain 1 · forest 1 · hill 2 · water ✕). Starting on a hill costs −1 move. Click green tile to move · click red-ringed foe to attack · Mend clicks a wounded ally (range 2) to heal 2. Each unit may move once + act once per turn, in any order.
plain  forest (+1 def = −1 dmg taken, min 1)  hill (+1 range, heavy footing)  water (impassable, blocks bow shots)
Blade: 12 HP · move 4 · 3 dmg melee(r1)  ·  Bow: 8 HP · move 3 · 2 dmg range 3  ·  Mend: 9 HP · move 3 · 1 dmg r1 · heal 2 (r2). Fog: foes visible only within 4 hexes of any ally — 👻 = last-known ghost.
