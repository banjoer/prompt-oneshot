❓ Rules & Help (pauses nothing — foes wait politely)

Goal

Clear 3 acts × 8 floors. HP persists. Death ends the run; score = floors×100 + kills×25 + gold.

Map

⚔ fight · 💀 elite (harder, better loot) · 🔥 rest (heal 30% or upgrade) · 🛒 shop (buy/remove) · 👹 boss. Click a glowing node connected to your position.

Combat

Each turn: gain 3⚡, draw to 5 cards. Block 🛡 resets at the start of your turn. Click a card, then a foe if it needs a target. End Turn to let foes act. Intent icons above foes show their next move (⚔ attack value · 🛡 block · ▲ buff · ▼ debuff · 🔥 burn).

Statuses

🔥 Burn: take N damage at start of turn, then Burn −1. 🌀 Weak: deal −25% damage (X turns). 💔 Frail: take +25% damage (X turns).

Cards

Strike 6 dmg · Guard 5 block · Sear applies Burn · Leech heals on hit · plus 12 more (Cleave hits ALL, Haste gives energy, etc). Upgraded cards (+ suffix) hit harder.

Seed

Run id in ?seed=NNN reproduces map + draft + foe moves. Enter a seed on the title screen or copy the URL mid-run.

Sound

WebAudio bleeps for hits/coins/cards.
