How to play

🧰 Palette (9+): belt ➡️, inserter ↘️ (picks BEHIND, drops AHEAD), miner ⛏️ (on ore), furnace 🔥 (2 ore→1 plate, 2s), assembler ⚙️ (tap to toggle gear: 2 plate→1 gear 4s / science: 2 plate+1 gear→1 pack 5s), pole ⚡ (radius 6), chest 📦, wall 🧱, turret 🔫, boiler ♨️ (power).

⚡ Power: boilers (4 MW each) → poles chain (≤6 tiles apart) → machines within 6 of a connected pole. Unpowered = ⚡ icon + stall. Watch MW readout.

🌫️ Pollution: furnaces/boilers/miners emit. Cloud diffuses, attracts biters from corner nests. Turrets (range 7) auto-fire when powered. Walls block. Minimap toggles overlays.

📱 Mobile gestures: tap build • drag pan • pinch zoom • double-tap rotate • long-press bulldoze • two-finger drag also pans. Toolbar swipes horizontally. R rotate, B bulldoze on desktop; wheel zooms, right-drag pans.

⬚ Select: SEL tool → drag box → COPY → PASTE tool tap to stamp. CUT deletes. Export gives base64 blueprint via clipboard.
