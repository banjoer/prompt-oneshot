❓ Rules & Touch Guide

📱 Touch gestures (mobile-first)

• Tap a card to select it, then tap a glowing foe to play an attack. Self-cards (Guard, Haste…) play on first tap.
• Swipe a card UP ☝️ to quick-play (self cards instantly; attacks select targeting mode).
• Drag & drop: press a card and drag it onto a foe, release to strike that foe — full hand gesture.
• Swipe DOWN 👇 on the battlefield / foe area to End Turn (or tap the big thumb button).
• Long-press (0.5s) a card to inspect full text; tap piles to view Draw/Discard.
• Map: swipe ↔️ to scroll, tap glowing node. Pinch is disabled so combat never zooms by accident. Haptics vibrate on hits when supported.

Goal

Clear 3 acts × 8 floors. HP persists. Death ends the run; score = floors×100 + kills×25 + gold.

Map

⚔ fight · 💀 elite (harder, better loot) · 🔥 rest (heal 30% or upgrade) · 🛒 shop (buy/remove) · 👹 boss. Tap a glowing node connected to your position.

Combat

Each turn: gain 3⚡, draw to 5 cards. Block 🛡 resets at the start of your turn. End Turn to let foes act. Intent icons above foes show next move (⚔ attack · 🛡 block · ▲ buff · ▼ debuff · 🔥 burn).

Statuses

🔥 Burn: take N at start of turn, then −1. 🌀 Weak: deal −25%. 💔 Frail: take +25%.

Cards

Strike 6 dmg · Guard 5 block · Sear Burn · Leech heal-on-hit · plus 12 more (Cleave hits ALL, Haste energy, etc). Upgraded (+) hit harder.

Seed

?seed=NNN reproduces map + draft + foe moves. Enter a seed on title or copy URL mid-run.
