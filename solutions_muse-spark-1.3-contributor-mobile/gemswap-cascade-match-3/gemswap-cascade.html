💎 Gemswap Cascade

Swap jewels into lines of 3+. Cascades multiply your combo. Match-4 forges a blaster & clears the whole row / column.

👆 Tap-tapTap one gem, then tap an orthogonally adjacent gem to swap.
👉 SwipeTouch a gem and flick up / down / left / right to swap instantly.
🔨 HammerArm the hammer, then tap any tile to smash it (once per game).
