📱 How to play on mobile — taps, touches, swipes & gestures
  • 👆 Tap a covered cell to reveal · tap an open number to chord.
  • 👇 Long-press (hold ~0.5s) a covered cell to flag / unflag — works in any mode, with vibration.
  • 🚩 Flag-mode switch: tap 🚩 Flag so every tap flags; tap 💣 Dig to go back. Good for one-handed play.
  • ✌️ Two-finger tap on the board toggles Dig / Flag mode.
  • ↔️ Swipe / drag on the board to pan (scroll) around big boards like Expert.
  • 🤏 Pinch on the board to zoom cells bigger/smaller (or use A− / A+).
  • 🖱️ Desktop: left-click reveal · right-click flag · click a satisfied number to chord.
