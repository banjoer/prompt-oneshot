❓ How to play

⌨️ Drive: ◀ ▶/A D steer, ▲/W gas, ▼/S brake/reverse, Space handbrake (drift), R respawn at last checkpoint (+5s), P pause, M mute.
📱 Touch buttons: hold ◀ ▶ to steer, hold GAS/BRK, HB = handbrake drift. All multi-touch.
👉 Swipe drive: enable “Swipe + auto-gas”, then touch & drag on the track: drag ◀ ▶ to steer, hold = auto-gas, drag ▼ = brake. Release = coast.
🤏 Hand gestures: 2-finger touch = handbrake · 3-finger tap = pause · 2-finger swipe down = respawn · double-tap canvas = handbrake pulse · pinch page still zooms menus (game canvas locked).
🏁 Rules: 3 laps, pass every quarter-checkpoint in order or the lap won’t count. Green = grass (slow). Red/white = kerb/wall (bounce + slowdown).
👻 Ghost: your best run per track is saved on-device and replayed translucently. Beat-by shows live ±m vs ghost.
🎮 Gamepad: left stick/d-pad steer, RT/A gas, LT/B brake, LB handbrake (if connected).
