Goal: grab keys (k), open doors (D), loot medkits (m) / shells (a) / scatter gun (W) / armor (v), kill foes, step on EXIT (E).
Foes: TROOPER hitscan rifle · RUSHER fast melee · SPITTER slow plasma.
They patrol → chase on sight → attack in range. Shooting them can trigger pain stagger; crossfire can make them hit each other (infighting).
Guns: BLASTER (∞) weak, fast. SCATTER (shells) 6 pellets, big damage. Max shells 24. Switch 1/2 / WPN / 2-finger swipe up.
