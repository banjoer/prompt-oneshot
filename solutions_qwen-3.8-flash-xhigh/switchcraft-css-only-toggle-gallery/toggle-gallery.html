⚡ Switchcraft

Six hand-crafted, CSS-only animated toggle switches. Click them, tab to them, press Space — and copy any switch's CSS with one button. No animation JavaScript, anywhere.

Classic Slide

A rounded pill track with a knob that slides across. The one every design system needs.

Neon Glow

A dark track that lights up cyan when on — glow, halo and all, purely from box-shadow.

Squishy

The knob stretches and squishes partway through the slide (@keyframes), then settles with elastic overshoot.

Day / Night

☀️ morphs into 🌙 as the knob slides, and the sky track crossfades to a starry night.

Pulse

Switch it on and the knob emits a repeating ripple ring — an endless @keyframes heartbeat.

Flip Lock

The knob rotates 180° in 3D (backface-visibility), revealing 🔒 on its hidden face.
